# Presentation Delivery

Presentation delivery combines voice, pauses, eye contact, posture, and spontaneous gesture to make a message easier to follow. Pauses provide structure and breathing room; they are preferable to repeated filler sounds.

Distribute eye contact across the audience rather than focusing only on familiar or responsive faces. In remote delivery, position the camera at eye level and look into it when addressing participants.

Practice helps turn normal speaking stress into usable energy. Preparation and knowledge of the topic support an audience-focused delivery.
