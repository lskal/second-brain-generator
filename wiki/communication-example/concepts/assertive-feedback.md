# Assertive Feedback

Assertive feedback describes an observable behavior, its effect, and a constructive preference. It avoids turning a specific, current issue into a judgment about another person's character.

For example, describe a concrete outcome and request rather than using an accusation. This keeps the conversation focused on a solvable situation and respects the other person's perspective.
