# Active Listening

Active listening gives the speaker evidence of attention while preserving space for their message to develop. Use appropriate eye contact, brief nonverbal feedback, and focused questions when clarification is needed.

Avoid preparing a response while the other person is still speaking, interrupting prematurely, or allowing a triggering word to replace the topic under discussion. Separate an observed event from a prior judgment about the person.
