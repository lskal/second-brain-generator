# Clear Communication

Effective communication aims for shared understanding, not compulsory agreement. A message is successful when the listener can understand its intended meaning, even if they ultimately disagree with its conclusion.

Make the message simple, specific, and appropriate for the audience. Keep attention on the current subject rather than on assumptions about the speaker or listener.
