# Communication and Public Speaking Course

## Summary

This course note presents practical guidance for communicating clearly, listening with attention, offering constructive feedback, speaking to groups, and building an argument. The compiled concepts retain the general techniques while avoiding personal examples and unsupported numerical claims from the notes.

## Key ideas

- [[clear-communication]] separates mutual understanding from persuading someone to agree.
- [[active-listening]] focuses attention, feedback, clarification, and withholding premature judgment.
- [[assertive-feedback]] addresses observable behavior rather than attacking a person.
- [[presentation-delivery]] uses voice, pauses, eye contact, gesture, and preparation to support the message.
- [[structured-argumentation]] organizes a thesis, evidence, counterarguments, and conclusion.
