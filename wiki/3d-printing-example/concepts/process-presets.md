# Process Presets

A process preset combines multiple slicer settings for a particular goal. Its layer-height number describes vertical resolution, while its label can indicate a different priority such as balanced everyday printing or functional strength.

A 0.20 mm Standard preset is a general balance of speed, appearance, reliability, and material use. A 0.20 mm Strength preset retains the same vertical resolution but may favor stronger walls, extrusion, infill, and interlayer adhesion. Fine presets such as 0.08 mm prioritize surface quality and detail, with substantially longer print times.

Select a preset by the part's purpose rather than assuming that a smaller layer height is universally better.
