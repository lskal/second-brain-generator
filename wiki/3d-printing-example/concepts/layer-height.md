# Layer Height

Layer height is the vertical thickness of each deposited FDM layer. A lower value creates more layers for the same object, which generally improves the appearance of curves and fine vertical detail at the cost of longer print times.

For example, 0.08 mm layers prioritize visual resolution compared with 0.20 mm layers. They do not automatically make a part stronger; functional strength depends more on walls, extrusion width, infill, and interlayer bonding.
