# Plane Cut

[[Bambu Studio]]'s Plane Cut operation splits a mesh along a movable, rotatable plane. It is suitable for clean planar changes such as shortening a part, removing a base, or splitting one model into printable sections.

Choose the useful side to retain when removing material. Choose to keep both sides when the goal is two separate objects rather than deletion.

Plane Cut is not a replacement for CAD or sculpting software. Curved cuts, shaped pockets, custom holes, and organic geometry changes require a modeling tool before slicing.
