# Bambu A1M Process Presets

## Summary

For a Bambu A1M using a 0.4 mm nozzle and PETG HF, preset names communicate both vertical resolution and intended printing priority. [[layer-height]] explains the numeric value, while [[process-presets]] distinguishes a balanced profile from strength- and appearance-oriented profiles.

## Selection guidance

- Use 0.20 mm Standard for general-purpose prints and prototypes.
- Use 0.20 mm Strength when functional durability is more important than speed or appearance alone.
- Use 0.08 mm fine-quality profiles when surface appearance and small details justify a longer print.
